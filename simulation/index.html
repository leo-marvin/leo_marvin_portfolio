    Run resultstolerance from the rules: 3 m

    Airframe and control

    6-DOF rigid-body model of the X500 (2.05 kg, 500 mm, thrust/weight 2.2) with motor lag, drag and gusting wind. ArduPilot-style cascade: position → velocity → attitude → body-rate PID → quad-X mixer.

    Mission

    Test #6 of the 2025–2026 rules: autonomous take-off from the LZ, fly to a GPS point and hold 10 s, fly a square of GPS points, hover until the jury calls Return To Home, land on the LZ.

    Perception

    Pinhole model of the Pi camera from the team's drone_config.yaml (f = 3.04 mm, 640×480). Each obstacle becomes a YOLO-style box with noise, misses and a confidence; boxes are tracked frame to frame.

    Decision

    The team's danger test: a box that grows fast between two frames is closing in (looming time-to-contact). Range comes from apparent size. Static obstacle → lateral detour at 3 m clearance; moving one → brake and yield.
